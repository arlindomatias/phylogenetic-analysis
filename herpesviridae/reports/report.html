This is a taxonomy-checking phylogeny, not an outbreak analysis. One RefSeq genome per virus was downloaded from NCBI Virus for the family Orthoherpesviridae (searched under its former name Herpesviridae), the orthologues of 8 core herpesvirus genes were found by homology to the HSV-1 reference, aligned, trimmed, concatenated into a partitioned supermatrix and used to infer a maximum likelihood tree. Every ICTV subfamily and genus was then tested for monophyly against that tree.

The headline result is that the classical subdivision of the family holds up: 3 of 3 subfamilies come out as clades, and 14 of 15 genera with two or more sampled viruses do as well (1 genus is represented by a single virus here and cannot be tested).

The family also contains the viruses with the strongest published link to cognitive decline in ageing, and 5 of them are in this tree: HSV-1 (the herpes–Alzheimer hypothesis), VZV, HCMV, EBV and HHV-6A. They are marked in the tree figure and discussed in their own section. The phylogeny says nothing about causation; it places those five viruses among their relatives, which is what a taxonomy analysis can legitimately contribute.

Genomes

The download is the RefSeq reference set of the family: 138 genomes covering 126 viruses and 17 genera, from 70 host species. RefSeq keeps one curated genome per virus, so the sample is non-redundant by construction — the right starting point for a taxonomic question, and the reason this project does not need to filter by sequence quality.

def plot_genome_size(meta_by_label, config, plots_dir):
    colors = config["colors"]
    groups = {}
    for row in meta_by_label.values():
        try:
            kb = int(row["genome_length"]) / 1000.0
        except (TypeError, ValueError):
            continue
        groups.setdefault(row["subfamily"], []).append((kb, row["organism"]))
    order = [s for s in ("Alphaherpesvirinae", "Betaherpesvirinae", "Gammaherpesvirinae")
             if s in groups] + [s for s in sorted(groups) if s not in
                                ("Alphaherpesvirinae", "Betaherpesvirinae", "Gammaherpesvirinae")]
    highlight = config.get("highlight", {})

    fig, ax = plt.subplots(figsize=(8, 4))
    rng = __import__("random").Random(config["seed"])
    for i, sub in enumerate(order):
        values = [v for v, _ in groups[sub]]
        ax.boxplot(values, positions=[i], widths=0.55, showfliers=False,
                   medianprops={"color": "#333333"})
        for kb, organism in groups[sub]:
            is_hl = organism in highlight
            ax.plot(i + rng.uniform(-0.17, 0.17), kb, marker="o",
                    ms=6 if is_hl else 3.4,
                    color=colors["highlight"] if is_hl else colors.get(sub, "#777777"),
                    alpha=1.0 if is_hl else 0.55, zorder=3 if is_hl else 2)
            if is_hl:
                ax.annotate(organism.replace("Human ", "H. "), (i, kb), fontsize=6.5,
                            xytext=(10, 0), textcoords="offset points", va="center",
                            color=colors["highlight"], fontweight="bold")
    ax.set_xticks(range(len(order)))
    ax.set_xticklabels([s.replace("herpesvirinae", "-") for s in order], fontsize=9)
    ax.set_ylabel("genome size (kb)")
    ax.set_title("Genome size by subfamily (RefSeq genomes in the tree)", fontsize=10, loc="left")
    ax.spines[["top", "right"]].set_visible(False)
    fig.tight_layout()
    save(fig, plots_dir, "genome_size_by_subfamily")

Marker genes

RefSeq protein names are not consistent across this family. Taking the product name of each HSV-1 query and counting the viruses that annotate a protein under exactly that name gives a median of 48% of the viruses per marker (range 43–64%), against 80–98% recovered by homology. So orthologues were not taken from the annotation text. Instead the 8 HSV-1 core proteins listed below were used as DIAMOND blastp queries against every protein of the family, and the best hit per virus passing an identity floor of 20% and a query coverage floor of 50% was kept. When one protein was the best hit of two markers in the same virus, it was assigned only to the higher-scoring marker, so no sequence enters the supermatrix twice.

Median identity to the query drops to around 26–30% for most markers, which is expected: the three subfamilies have been diverging for roughly 400 million years. That is also why UL26 (capsid maturation protease) was dropped from the set — its homologues align with the HSV-1 query only over the protease domain and failed the coverage floor in most beta- and gammaherpesviruses.

def plot_coverage(coverage, plots_dir):
    rows = sorted(coverage, key=lambda r: int(r["n_taxa"]))
    names = [r["marker"] for r in rows]
    taxa = [int(r["n_taxa"]) for r in rows]
    ident = [float(r["median_identity_to_query"]) for r in rows]

    fig, axes = plt.subplots(1, 2, figsize=(10, 3.6), sharey=True)
    axes[0].barh(names, taxa, color="#3399FF")
    for i, v in enumerate(taxa):
        axes[0].text(v + 1, i, str(v), va="center", fontsize=8)
    axes[0].set_xlabel("viruses with an orthologue")
    axes[0].set_title("Marker coverage", fontsize=10, loc="left")

    axes[1].barh(names, ident, color="#009933")
    for i, v in enumerate(ident):
        axes[1].text(v + 0.4, i, f"{v:.0f}%", va="center", fontsize=8)
    axes[1].set_xlabel("median identity to the HSV-1 query (%)")
    axes[1].set_title("Divergence from the query", fontsize=10, loc="left")
    for ax in axes:
        ax.spines[["top", "right"]].set_visible(False)
        ax.tick_params(labelsize=8)
    fig.tight_layout()
    save(fig, plots_dir, "marker_coverage")

Supermatrix

Each marker was aligned with MAFFT (--auto) and trimmed with trimAl (-gt 0.5), then the 8 trimmed alignments were concatenated into a supermatrix of 107 viruses × 7,547 columns with one partition per gene. A virus entered the matrix with at least 5 of the 8 markers; 81 viruses have all 8, and the median share of missing data per virus is 4.2%. The 9 viruses that fell below the threshold are mostly partial RefSeq entries whose genomes do not cover the core gene set.

Maximum likelihood tree

IQ-TREE 2 was run on the partitioned supermatrix with LG+F+G4 per partition, 1000 ultrafast bootstrap replicates and 1000 SH-aLRT replicates (seed 42). Reported log-likelihood of the tree: -693531.2266. The tree below is midpoint-rooted for display only: with no outgroup outside the family, the root position within Orthoherpesviridae is not identified by these data. The monophyly tests in the next section are therefore run on bipartitions, which do not depend on rooting.

def plot_tree(tree, meta_by_label, config, plots_dir):
    colors = config["colors"]
    highlight = config.get("highlight", {})
    x, y = layout(tree)
    n_tips = sum(1 for _ in tree.leaf_node_iter())

    fig, ax = plt.subplots(figsize=(13, max(8, 0.19 * n_tips)))

    def subfamily_of(node):
        subs = {meta_by_label[lf.taxon.label]["subfamily"] for lf in node.leaf_iter()}
        return subs.pop() if len(subs) == 1 else None

    for node in tree.preorder_node_iter():
        sub = subfamily_of(node)
        color = colors.get(sub, "#555555") if sub else "#999999"
        if node.parent_node is not None:
            ax.plot([x[node.parent_node], x[node]], [y[node], y[node]],
                    color=color, lw=1.0, solid_capstyle="round", zorder=2)
        children = list(node.child_node_iter())
        if children:
            ys = [y[c] for c in children]
            ax.plot([x[node], x[node]], [min(ys), max(ys)], color=color, lw=1.0, zorder=2)
            try:                                  # IQ-TREE label: 'SH-aLRT/UFboot'
                ufboot = float(str(node.label).split("/")[1])
            except (AttributeError, IndexError, ValueError):
                ufboot = None
            if ufboot is not None and ufboot >= UFBOOT_CUTOFF:
                ax.plot([x[node]], [y[node]], marker="o", ms=2.6, color="#333333", zorder=3)

    xmax = max(x.values())
    for leaf in tree.leaf_node_iter():
        label = leaf.taxon.label
        row = meta_by_label[label]
        organism = row["organism"][:1].upper() + row["organism"][1:]  # NCBI mixes capitalisation
        note = highlight.get(organism)
        color = colors.get(row["subfamily"], colors.get("unclassified", "#777777"))
        ax.plot([x[leaf], xmax * 1.015], [y[leaf], y[leaf]], color="#dddddd", lw=0.4, zorder=1)
        ax.text(xmax * 1.02, y[leaf], organism, fontsize=5.6, va="center",
                color=colors["highlight"] if note else color,
                fontweight="bold" if note else "normal")
        if note:
            ax.text(xmax * 1.52, y[leaf], "◀ " + note, fontsize=5.6, va="center",
                    color=colors["highlight"], fontweight="bold")

    bar = round(xmax / 10, 2) or xmax / 10
    ax.plot([0, bar], [-2.2, -2.2], color="#333333", lw=1.4)
    ax.text(bar / 2, -3.4, f"{bar} subst./site", ha="center", fontsize=7, color="#333333")

    handles = [Line2D([], [], color=colors[s], lw=2, label=s)
               for s in ("Alphaherpesvirinae", "Betaherpesvirinae", "Gammaherpesvirinae")]
    handles.append(Line2D([], [], color="#333333", marker="o", ms=4, lw=0,
                          label=f"ultrafast bootstrap ≥ {UFBOOT_CUTOFF}%"))
    handles.append(Line2D([], [], color=colors["highlight"], lw=2,
                          label="linked to cognitive decline in ageing"))
    ax.legend(handles=handles, loc="lower right", frameon=False, fontsize=7.5)

    ax.set_xlim(-xmax * 0.02, xmax * 2.25)
    ax.set_ylim(-5, n_tips + 1)
    ax.axis("off")
    ax.set_title("Orthoherpesviridae: maximum likelihood tree of 8 core genes\n"
                 "midpoint-rooted; tips coloured by ICTV subfamily", fontsize=11, loc="left")
    save(fig, plots_dir, "tree_subfamily")

The human herpesviruses linked to cognitive decline in ageing

Of the 10 human-derived viruses in the tree, 5 carry a published association with cognitive decline or dementia in ageing. They do not form a clade — they are spread across all three subfamilies, which is the point worth taking from this analysis: the phenotype that connects them is a property of persistent infection of a long-lived host, not of a single viral lineage.

  • HSV-1 (Simplexvirus, Alphaherpesvirinae) is the virus of the herpes–Alzheimer hypothesis: latency in the trigeminal ganglion, reactivation, and the APOE-ε4 interaction.
  • VZV (Varicellovirus, Alphaherpesvirinae), the sister lineage in the same subfamily, is linked to dementia risk through zoster and vasculopathy, and the vaccination studies.
  • HCMV (Cytomegalovirus) and HHV-6A (Roseolovirus) sit in Betaherpesvirinae, the subfamily with the largest genomes; HCMV is the main driver of immunosenescence in ageing cohorts.
  • EBV (Lymphocryptovirus, Gammaherpesvirinae) is the most distant of the five.

Limits

  • One genome per virus. RefSeq gives a clean taxonomic sample, but it says nothing about variation inside a species; the HSV-1 tip here stands for strain 17, not for the 197 complete HSV-1 genomes available in NCBI Virus.
  • Eight genes, not whole genomes. Core genes are what can be aligned across 400 million years of divergence. Gene content, gene order and the large subfamily-specific gene families carry further signal that this matrix ignores.
  • No root. Without an outgroup (the fish and amphibian herpesviruses of Alloherpesviridae are too divergent for these alignments), the root inside the family is not identified, and the deepest relationships among the three subfamilies should be read as unresolved rather than as drawn.
  • No dating. Branch lengths are substitutions per site, not time.
  • Association, not causation. The notes on cognitive decline summarise the epidemiological and neuropathological literature on these viruses; nothing in this phylogeny tests them.
